---
name: morning
description: Start-of-day ritual. Opens or creates today's note, reads new files in drop/, checks todo.md for overdue items, and helps decide what to work on.
---

Check if daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md exists for today.

If yes, read it and give a quick status.

If no, create it:

# [Today's Date]

## Working On

## Loose Ends

## Scratch Pad

Then:
- Look in drop/ for anything that arrived since yesterday — read it (PDFs, images, whatever)
- Check todo.md for overdue items or anything tagged `!due:` today
- Skim the most recently touched folder under projects/ for context
- Highlight the 2-3 things that seem most pressing
- End with: "What do you want to tackle first?"
